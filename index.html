My Process

From idea to launch: I take care of the full web development life cycle.

  1. 01

    Plan

    Understand your goals, users, and features, then agree on the scope and timeline.

  2. 02

    Design

    Sketch the layout and create a clean, responsive interface that is easy to use.

  3. 03

    Develop

    Build the front end with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and React, and the back end with Node.js, Express.js, and MongoDB.

  4. 04

    Test

    Check every page, form, and screen size, and fix bugs before anyone else finds them.

  5. 05

    Deploy

    Publish the site online with GitHub Pages or Render, ready for real visitors.

  6. 06

    Maintain

    Keep it updated, secure, and improving after launch.
