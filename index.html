My Process

Like a maple leaf, every project has a life cycle: from a small bud to its brightest colours, and on to the next season. I take care of every stage.

  1. 01

    Plan

    BudUnderstand your goals, users, and features, then agree on the scope and timeline.

  2. 02

    Design

    Young leafSketch the layout and create a clean, responsive interface that is easy to use.

  3. 03

    Develop

    Mature leafBuild the front end with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and React, and the back end with Node.js, Express.js, and MongoDB.

  4. 04

    Test

    TurningCheck every page, form, and screen size, and fix bugs before anyone else finds them.

  5. 05

    Deploy

    Peak colourPublish it online with GitHub Pages or Render, or on AWS: S3 for static sites, EC2 for servers.

  6. 06

    Maintain

    Falling leafKeep it updated, secure, and improving after launch, ready for the next season.
